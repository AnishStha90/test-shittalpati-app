Sudurpaschim Province News

Kailali: Flood-hit families to get aid

Kailali, Sudurpaschim Province - The Sudurpaschim Province government has announced that it will provide financial assistance to families affected by the recent floods in the region.

The government has allocated NPR 10 million for the relief efforts, which will be distributed among the affected families.

Baitadi: New road to connect district with Nepal-India border

Baitadi, Sudurpaschim Province - The construction of a new road in Baitadi district is expected to connect the district with the Nepal-India border, boosting trade and commerce in the region.

The road, which is being constructed at a cost of NPR 50 million, will also improve connectivity between the district and other parts of the province.

Doti: Farmers protest against low prices of crops

Doti, Sudurpaschim Province - Farmers in Doti district have been protesting against the low prices of crops in the region.

The farmers are demanding that the government increase the prices of crops to make farming a viable profession.
