Question to Prof. Dr. Bhola Thapa

Your appointment was through meritocracy, but others’ how?

Prof. Dr. Bhola Thapa, the newly appointed Vice-Chancellor of Tribhuvan University, has been at the center of controversy after his appointment was made public. While Prof. Thapa's appointment was made through a merit-based selection process, many are questioning the appointments of others in the university.

According to sources, several high-ranking officials in the university were appointed without any competition or merit-based selection process. This has raised questions about the fairness and transparency of the appointment process.

When asked about the appointments, Prof. Thapa said that he was not aware of the details of the appointments and that he would look into the matter.

The controversy surrounding Prof. Thapa's appointment has sparked a heated debate about the need for transparency and accountability in the university's appointment process.
