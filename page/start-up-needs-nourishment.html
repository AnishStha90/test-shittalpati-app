Start-up needs nourishment

Read More

Start-ups are the backbone of any economy, but they require nourishment to grow. A lack of funding can be detrimental to their growth and may lead to their demise.

Investors play a crucial role in providing the necessary funding to start-ups. They not only provide financial support but also mentorship and guidance to help start-ups navigate the complex business landscape.

However, the current state of start-ups in Nepal is concerning. Many start-ups are struggling to secure funding, and those that do manage to secure funding often find themselves struggling to sustain themselves.

It is essential for investors to come forward and provide the necessary support to start-ups. This will not only help start-ups grow but also contribute to the overall growth of the economy.
