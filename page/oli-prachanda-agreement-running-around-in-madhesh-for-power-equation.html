Oli-Prachanda agreement: Running around in Madhesh for power equation

July 26, 2026

Leaders of the CPN-UML and CPN-Maoist Centre have reached an agreement to form an alliance to take on the Nepali Congress in the upcoming elections.

According to sources, the agreement was reached after a series of meetings between the two parties' top leaders.

The alliance is expected to give a boost to the Left forces in the country and create a strong challenge to the Nepali Congress.

The agreement comes at a time when the country is gearing up for the local level elections.

The elections are expected to be held in the next few months.
