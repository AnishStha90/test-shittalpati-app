The cold storage of Nawalparasi, neglect by the province: farmers' storage in traditional style

Locals of Nawalparasi have been storing their crops in traditional style, despite the availability of modern cold storage facilities.

The farmers say that they prefer the traditional method as it is more cost-effective and environmentally friendly.

However, the local government has been criticized for its neglect in providing adequate cold storage facilities to the farmers.

Experts say that the lack of modern cold storage facilities is affecting the quality and shelf life of the crops.

The issue has sparked a debate among the locals, with some calling for the government to take immediate action to address the issue.
