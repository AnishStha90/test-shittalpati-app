The principal of the school, Bhandari, was punched

July 26, 2024

The principal of the school, Bhandari, was punched by a student in a shocking incident that has left the entire school community in shock.

According to eyewitnesses, the incident occurred during a heated argument between the principal and the student.

The student, who has been identified as 17-year-old Rohan, has been taken into custody by the police.

The principal, who has been hospitalized for treatment, is said to be in stable condition.

The school administration has condemned the incident and has promised to take strict action against the student involved.

The police are investigating the incident and have promised to take action against anyone found guilty.
