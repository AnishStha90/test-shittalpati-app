Madan Bhandari Birth Anniversary: Oli, Prachanda, and Nepal on same stage

Top leaders gather to pay tribute to the late CPN (ML) leader

Published on: 2024-02-12

Kathmandu, Nepal - In a rare display of unity, top leaders of Nepal's major political parties, including Prime Minister KP Sharma Oli and CPN (Maoist Centre) Chairman Pushpa Kamal Dahal Prachanda, came together on stage to pay tribute to the late CPN (Marxist Leninist) leader Madan Bhandari on his birth anniversary.

The event, held at the party's central office in Kathmandu, was attended by hundreds of party cadres and supporters, who gathered to pay their respects to the late leader.

In his speech, PM Oli praised Bhandari's contributions to the country's politics and his role in shaping the party's ideology.

Prachanda, on the other hand, remembered Bhandari as a "true revolutionary" who dedicated his life to the cause of socialism and communism.

The event was a rare display of unity among Nepal's top leaders, who have been engaged in a bitter power struggle in recent months.
