In Lumbini, 20 percent of the farmland remains to be planted

Published: January 1, 2024

According to recent data, 20 percent of the farmland in Lumbini remains unplanted.

The statistics were released by the Lumbini Agricultural Development Office, highlighting the need for increased agricultural production in the region.

Experts say that the remaining farmland needs to be planted with crops such as rice, wheat, and maize to meet the growing demand for food in the region.

By: Rajendra KC, Lumbini Correspondent
