Full text of the Supreme Court order: Do not discriminate in government advertisements

Published: Today, 10:00 AM

The Supreme Court has issued an order to the government to refrain from discriminating against certain groups in government advertisements.

According to the order, the government must ensure that all advertisements are fair and do not target any particular group.

The court also directed the government to take necessary measures to prevent any form of discrimination in the future.

The full text of the order is as follows:

"The government shall ensure that all advertisements are fair and do not target any particular group. The government shall also take necessary measures to prevent any form of discrimination in the future."

The Supreme Court's order is a significant development in the country's efforts to promote equality and prevent discrimination.

The government has been directed to comply with the order within a specified timeframe.
