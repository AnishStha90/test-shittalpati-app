If the agreement with the meter interest victims is not implemented, we will go back to the movement: Awadhesh Kushwaha

Awadhesh Kushwaha, a key figure in the meter interest movement, has warned that if the government fails to implement the agreement reached with the victims, the movement will resume.

Speaking to the media, Kushwaha emphasized the importance of implementing the agreement, which aims to provide relief to those affected by the meter interest issue.

The movement, which gained momentum in recent months, was suspended after the government agreed to negotiate with the victims. However, Kushwaha's statement suggests that the movement may resume if the agreement is not implemented.

Kushwaha's warning has sparked concerns among government officials, who are working to implement the agreement. The government has promised to provide relief to the victims, but the process has been slow.

As the situation unfolds, the public is watching closely to see if the government will implement the agreement and provide relief to the victims.
