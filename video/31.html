To sustain in business, long-term planning and patience are needed: Shanta Thapa

Managing Director of De Crown

Shanta Thapa, the Managing Director of De Crown, has emphasized the importance of long-term planning and patience in sustaining a business.

According to Thapa, businesses often face numerous challenges, including economic downturns, changes in consumer behavior, and intense competition.

However, he believes that by adopting a long-term perspective and being patient, entrepreneurs can navigate these challenges and achieve success.

Thapa's remarks come at a time when many businesses in Nepal are struggling to stay afloat due to various factors, including the COVID-19 pandemic and economic uncertainty.

As the Managing Director of De Crown, Thapa has firsthand experience of the challenges faced by businesses in Nepal.

Under his leadership, De Crown has been able to sustain itself and even expand its operations, despite the challenging business environment.
