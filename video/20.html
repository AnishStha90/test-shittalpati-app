Shikshyalaya | Nayagaun School excels in Sajitama

Nayagaun School, a renowned institution in the heart of Kathmandu, has once again proven its excellence in the field of education. The school's students have made a remarkable achievement in the Sajitama, a prestigious national-level competition.

Competition Details

The Sajitama competition is a highly competitive event that attracts top talent from all over the country. This year's competition saw a record number of participants, making it even more challenging for the students to stand out.

Shikshyalaya's Achievements

The students of Nayagaun School, under the guidance of their dedicated teachers, worked tirelessly to prepare for the competition. Their hard work paid off as they secured top positions in various categories.

  • First Prize in Science Category: Rajeshwor Timilsina
  • Second Prize in Mathematics Category: Srijana Poudel
  • Third Prize in English Category: Aashish Thapa

Principal's Message

"We are extremely proud of our students' achievements in the Sajitama competition. Their dedication and perseverance are a testament to the school's commitment to providing quality education. We look forward to seeing our students excel in their future endeavors."
