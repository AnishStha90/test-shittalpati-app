One person is missing after being swept away in the flood in Dolakha, and four people are trapped on a cliff

July 26, 2024

Dolakha Flood

One person is missing after being swept away in the flood in Dolakha, and four people are trapped on a cliff. The incident occurred on Tuesday evening when the swollen river swept away the person, who is yet to be identified.

The four people trapped on the cliff are feared to be in a critical condition. The rescue operation is underway, and the authorities are working to rescue them safely.

The flood has caused widespread damage in the region, with many homes and businesses affected. The authorities have warned of more flooding in the coming days and have asked people to be vigilant.
