FIFA Suspends ANFA Amidst Ongoing World Cup

December 5, 2022

FIFA has suspended the All Nepal Football Association (ANFA) with immediate effect, citing "undue influence" and "third-party interference" in the country's football affairs.

The decision comes as a shock to the Nepali football community, which was eagerly following the ongoing FIFA World Cup in Qatar.

ANFA President Kumar Thapa has been at the center of controversy for some time now, with allegations of corruption and mismanagement levelled against him.

FIFA has given ANFA 45 days to respond to the suspension, after which the decision will be reviewed.

In the meantime, the Nepali football team will not be able to participate in any international tournaments, including the upcoming SAFF Championship.
