Samrajyee, who has made an acting 'comeback' after two years, says: The Nepali cinema sector has become good.

He expressed his views in an interview with our team.

When asked about the current state of Nepali cinema, he said, "The Nepali cinema sector has become good."

He further added, "I am glad to see the growth of Nepali cinema."

Samrajyee's comeback film is set to release soon.
