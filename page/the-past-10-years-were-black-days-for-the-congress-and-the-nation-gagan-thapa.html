The past 10 years were black days for the Congress and the nation: Gagan Thapa

Nepali Congress leader Gagan Thapa has said that the past decade has been a dark period for the party and the nation.

Speaking at a programme organised by the party's youth wing, Thapa said that the party had failed to live up to the expectations of the people and that it had been unable to address the country's pressing issues.

He also said that the party had been plagued by internal conflicts and that it had been unable to present a united front to the people.

Thapa's comments come at a time when the party is facing a major crisis, with many of its leaders and cadres questioning its leadership and direction.

The party's youth wing has been at the forefront of the party's efforts to rejuvenate itself and to connect with the younger generation.

Thapa's speech was seen as a major boost to the party's efforts to revive itself and to present a united front to the people.

However, the party still faces a major challenge in terms of its leadership and direction, and it remains to be seen whether it will be able to overcome its current crisis.
