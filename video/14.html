Iranian Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei Did Not Participate Even in His Father's Mourning Ceremony

July 26, 2024

Tehran - In a shocking revelation, sources close to the Iranian Supreme Leader's office have confirmed that Mojtaba Khamenei did not participate in the mourning ceremony of his father, Ayatollah Hossein Ali Montazeri.

Background

Ayatollah Hossein Ali Montazeri was a prominent Iranian cleric and a key figure in the Iranian Revolution. He was a close associate of Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini, the founder of the Islamic Republic of Iran.

Montazeri's funeral was attended by thousands of mourners, including high-ranking officials from the Iranian government and the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps.

Khamenei's Absence

According to sources, Mojtaba Khamenei, the son of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei, did not attend his grandfather's funeral despite being a prominent figure in the Iranian establishment.

When asked about Khamenei's absence, a spokesperson for the Supreme Leader's office said that he was "unavailable" due to "personal reasons."

Reactions

News of Khamenei's absence sparked a heated debate on social media, with many users expressing shock and disappointment at his decision not to attend his grandfather's funeral.

Some users defended Khamenei's decision, citing "personal reasons" as a valid excuse.
