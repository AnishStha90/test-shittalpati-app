Opium and cannabis cultivation destroyed on 56 ropani of land in Rolpa

Crime News

Published: 10th February 2024

Security personnel have destroyed opium and cannabis cultivation on 56 ropani of land in Rolpa district.

A team of security personnel, led by the District Administration Office, Rolpa, conducted the operation in the early hours of the morning.

The operation was carried out in the areas of Jhyau and Gauriphanta in Rolpa district.

According to the District Administration Office, Rolpa, the operation was conducted based on intelligence inputs.

The security personnel seized a total of 1,500 kilograms of opium and cannabis during the operation.

The seized items were destroyed on the spot.
