Extreme Torture in Butwal Correctional Home

Young Woman on Ventilator, 4 Suspects Arrested

A young woman is on a ventilator after being subjected to extreme torture at the Butwal Correctional Home. The incident has sparked outrage and calls for action from authorities. In a related development, four suspects have been arrested in connection with the case.

According to eyewitnesses, the young woman was brutally beaten and subjected to inhumane treatment by the correctional home staff. She was later rushed to a nearby hospital, where she is currently on a ventilator.
