Three Years of Tormented Microfinance Victims

A couple, tormented for three years, asked, "Will we get justice now?"

A couple, who have been suffering for three years, finally found the courage to speak out against the microfinance institution that has been tormenting them.

The couple, who wish to remain anonymous, have been struggling to make ends meet since they took a loan from the microfinance institution. Despite their best efforts, they have been unable to pay back the loan, and the institution has been harassing them relentlessly.

The couple's story is not an isolated one. There are many others who have been victimized by microfinance institutions in Nepal. The government has been criticized for not doing enough to protect these victims.

As the couple's case highlights, the lack of regulation and oversight in the microfinance sector has led to widespread exploitation of vulnerable individuals. It is high time for the government to take concrete steps to address this issue and provide justice to the victims.
