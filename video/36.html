Madheshi parties unite against repealing Provincial Assembly, warn of protest !

Political News

Kathmandu, [Current Date] - In a significant development, Madheshi parties have come together to protest against the government's decision to repeal the Provincial Assembly.

The parties, which have been vocal about their opposition to the move, have warned of widespread protests if the decision is not reversed.

According to sources, the parties have been in talks with the government to find a solution, but so far, no breakthrough has been achieved.

The Madheshi parties have been a key player in Nepal's politics for decades, and their opposition to the government's decision is seen as a major challenge for the ruling party.

The situation is being closely watched by observers, who are waiting to see how the government will respond to the parties' demands.
