FIFA World Cup: Semi-finals to feature clash of former champions

July 26, 2026

The highly anticipated FIFA World Cup 2026 semi-finals are set to feature a thrilling clash between two former champions. In a mouth-watering encounter, reigning champions Brazil will take on 2002 World Cup winners France in a battle of titans.

The two teams have a rich history of producing some of the most iconic moments in World Cup history. Brazil's mesmerizing 1970 team, led by the legendary Pelé, is still etched in the memories of football fans worldwide.

On the other hand, France's 1998 World Cup triumph under the guidance of Aimé Jacquet will always be remembered for its dramatic finale against Brazil.

This year's semi-final clash promises to be a repeat of the 2001 FIFA Confederations Cup final, where Brazil edged out France 5-4 in a penalty shootout.

Match Details

  • Date: July 28, 2026
  • Time: 20:00 IST
  • Stadium: Lusail Iconic Stadium, Qatar
  • Referee: Szymon Marciniak (Poland)
