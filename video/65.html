Devi's Fall ( पाताले छाँगो )

A Breathtaking Tourist Attraction in Pokhara

Devi's Fall, also known as Patale Chango, is a popular tourist destination in Pokhara, Nepal. Located near the Phewa Lake, this stunning waterfall is a must-visit for anyone traveling to Nepal.
