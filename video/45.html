Nepal Requests Saudi Arabia to Halt Skill Testing Program

Nepal has requested Saudi Arabia to halt its skill testing program for Nepali migrant workers.

The request was made by the Nepali government after several Nepali workers were reportedly subjected to inhumane treatment during the skill testing process.

The Saudi Arabian government has been conducting skill tests for Nepali workers as part of its efforts to streamline the recruitment process and ensure that only skilled workers are employed in the country.

However, several Nepali workers have reported being subjected to physical and mental torture during the skill testing process, which has led to widespread outrage in Nepal.

The Nepali government has now requested the Saudi Arabian government to halt the skill testing program until further notice.

The move is seen as a major victory for the Nepali workers who have been fighting against the inhumane treatment meted out to them by the Saudi Arabian authorities.

The Nepali government has also requested the Saudi Arabian government to provide compensation to the workers who have been subjected to inhumane treatment during the skill testing process.
