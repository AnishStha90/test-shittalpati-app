Breaking News

21 Ashar 2083

Butwal, 21 Ashar - In a shocking turn of events, a massive fire broke out at the local market in Butwal today, leaving several shops and stalls damaged.

According to eyewitnesses, the fire started around 10 am and quickly spread to nearby shops, causing panic among the crowd.

The fire was brought under control by the local fire brigade, but not before several shops were reduced to ashes.

The cause of the fire is still unknown, but an investigation has been launched to determine the reason behind the blaze.

Meanwhile, the local authorities have assured that they will provide all necessary assistance to the affected shopkeepers and traders.
