Foreigners have vulture eyes on Nepal: Nabina Lama

By [Author Name]

Nepal has long been a subject of interest for foreigners, with many eyeing the country's natural resources and strategic location.

Renowned journalist Nabina Lama recently expressed her concerns about the growing interest of foreigners in Nepal, stating that it poses a significant threat to the country's sovereignty.

Lama's comments come at a time when Nepal is facing increased pressure from its neighbors and foreign powers to open up its economy and natural resources.

The government has been under pressure to implement policies that favor foreign investment and trade, which has raised concerns among locals about the potential loss of control over the country's resources.

Lama's warning is a timely reminder of the need for Nepal to be cautious about its dealings with foreign powers and to prioritize its own interests.

As the country navigates its complex relationships with its neighbors and foreign powers, it is essential to remain vigilant and protect its sovereignty.

Only time will tell if Nepal can successfully navigate this delicate balance and emerge stronger and more resilient.
