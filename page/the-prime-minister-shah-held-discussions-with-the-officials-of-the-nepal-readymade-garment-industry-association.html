The Prime Minister Shah held discussions with the officials of the Nepal Readymade Garment Industry Association

Kathmandu, 20th July 2024

The Prime Minister of Nepal, Mr. Shah, held a meeting with the officials of the Nepal Readymade Garment Industry Association today.

The meeting aimed at discussing the current issues and challenges faced by the garment industry in Nepal.

The Prime Minister assured the officials that his government would provide necessary support to the industry to overcome the challenges.
