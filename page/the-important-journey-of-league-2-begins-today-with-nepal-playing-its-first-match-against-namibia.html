The important journey of League-2 begins today, with Nepal playing its first match against Namibia.

July 26, 2024

League-2, the prestigious international football tournament, is all set to kick off today with Nepal taking on Namibia in its opening match.

The Nepali team has been preparing for this moment for months, and the players are all set to give their best on the field.

The match will be broadcast live on our radio station, so tune in to catch all the action.
