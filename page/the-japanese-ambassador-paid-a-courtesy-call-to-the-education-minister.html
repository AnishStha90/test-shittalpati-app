The Japanese Ambassador paid a courtesy call to the Education Minister

The Japanese Ambassador to Nepal, Mr. Shigemitsu Motomiya, paid a courtesy call to the Education Minister, Hon. Devendra Paudel, at the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology.

The meeting was held in a cordial atmosphere, with both sides exchanging views on various aspects of bilateral cooperation in the field of education.

The Education Minister expressed his gratitude to the Japanese Ambassador for the continued support and cooperation extended by the Government of Japan to the education sector in Nepal.

The meeting was attended by senior officials from the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology and the Embassy of Japan in Nepal.

Source: Embassy of Japan in Nepal
