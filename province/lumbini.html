Govt. to Construct New Road in Rupandehi

The government has announced plans to construct a new road connecting Rupandehi and Nawalparasi districts.

The road, which will be a 20-kilometer stretch, is expected to improve connectivity between the two districts and boost economic activities in the region.

According to sources, the construction work is expected to begin soon and will be completed within the next two years.

Farmer's Market to be Established in Kapilvastu

The Kapilvastu district administration has announced plans to establish a farmer's market in the district.

The market, which will be a one-stop shop for farmers to sell their produce, is expected to improve the livelihoods of farmers in the region.

According to sources, the market will be established soon and will provide a platform for farmers to sell their produce directly to consumers.

Health Camp Organized in Arghakhanchi

A health camp was organized in Arghakhanchi district, providing free medical services to locals.

The health camp, which was organized by a local NGO, provided free medical services to over 500 locals.

According to sources, the health camp was a huge success and will be organized again in the future.
