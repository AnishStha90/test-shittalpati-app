The Congress Koshi meeting ended inconclusively: the issue of withdrawal from the government remains undecided.

Kathmandu, 22 Shrawan 2078

Leaders of the CPN (Unified Socialist) have failed to reach a consensus on the issue of withdrawing from the government during the party's central committee meeting held in Koshi.

Although the meeting was expected to decide on the party's future course, the leaders were unable to reach a consensus on the issue.

The party's central committee meeting was held in Koshi from 19 to 22 Shrawan.

The meeting was attended by party leaders, including party chairperson Madhav Kumar Nepal and general secretary Sunil Chaudhary.

However, the party's central committee meeting ended inconclusively.
