About Us

Shittal Pati, Nepal's premier English and Nepali FM radio station, has been serving the nation with quality news, entertainment, and information since its inception in 2001.

Our Mission

To provide accurate, unbiased, and timely news and information to the people of Nepal, promoting transparency, accountability, and good governance.

Our Values

  • Integrity: We uphold the highest standards of ethics and morality in our journalism.
  • Objectivity: We strive to present a balanced view of events, free from personal biases and agendas.
  • Accuracy: We verify facts and sources to ensure the accuracy of our reports.
  • Accountability: We take responsibility for our actions and are transparent in our decision-making processes.
