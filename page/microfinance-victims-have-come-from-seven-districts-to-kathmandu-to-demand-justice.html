Microfinance victims have come from seven districts to Kathmandu to demand justice

July 26, 2024

Protesters from seven districts of Nepal have gathered in Kathmandu to demand justice for the victims of microfinance institutions.

The protesters, who have been camping in the capital city for several days, are demanding that the government take immediate action against the microfinance institutions that have been accused of exploiting and cheating their customers.

The protesters have also been demanding that the government provide compensation to the victims and their families.

The government has promised to take action against the microfinance institutions, but the protesters are demanding that the government take immediate action and not just make promises.
