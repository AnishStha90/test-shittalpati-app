Iranian Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei Did Not Participate Even in His Father's Funeral Ceremony

Iranian Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei did not participate in the funeral ceremony of his father, however, the reason behind his absence is not clear yet.

According to reports, Mojtaba Khamenei was not present at the funeral ceremony, which has raised several questions among the public.

The Iranian government has not made any official statement regarding the Supreme Leader's absence from the funeral ceremony.

As the news of Mojtaba Khamenei's absence from the funeral ceremony spreads, the public is eagerly waiting for an official statement from the government.
