FIFA President Infantino's aerial journey throughout the World Cup: 95,000 km travel, 5 days in the air

A look at the grueling travel schedule of the FIFA President

According to recent reports, FIFA President Infantino has embarked on an extensive travel schedule throughout the World Cup, covering a staggering 95,000 kilometers in just five days.

Infantino's aerial journey has taken him to various host cities, where he has been meeting with officials, attending matches, and engaging with fans.

The sheer scale of his travel schedule has raised eyebrows, with many questioning the feasibility and necessity of such extensive travel.
