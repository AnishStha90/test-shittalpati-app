CPN-UML lawmaker Koiri sentenced to 5 years in prison

A Kathmandu district court has sentenced CPN-UML lawmaker Bimalendra Koiri to five years in prison in a corruption case.

Koiri, who is also the vice-chairman of the CPN-UML, was found guilty of amassing assets worth over Rs 1.5 crore through corrupt means.

The court also ordered Koiri to pay a fine of Rs 50,000 and to return the ill-gotten assets.

The verdict was delivered by Judge Surya Prasad Upadhyay on Monday.

Koiri's lawyer has said that they will appeal the verdict in the Supreme Court.
