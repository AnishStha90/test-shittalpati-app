Extreme torture at Butwal reform home, girl in ventilator, 4 accused arrested

Breaking News

A 16-year-old girl is currently in the ventilator at a hospital in Butwal after being subjected to extreme torture at the local reform home. The incident has sparked widespread outrage and protests in the region.

The girl was allegedly tortured by the staff members of the reform home, which has been accused of inhumane treatment of its inmates. The police have arrested four accused in connection with the incident.

The reform home has been in the news for all the wrong reasons in the past. There have been several complaints of physical and mental abuse of the inmates by the staff members.

The government has promised to take stern action against the perpetrators and ensure that such incidents do not happen in the future.

The incident has raised questions about the functioning of the reform homes and the need for better monitoring and regulation.
