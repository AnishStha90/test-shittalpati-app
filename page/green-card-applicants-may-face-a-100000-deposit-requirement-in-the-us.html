Green card applicants may face a $100,000 deposit requirement in the US.

July 26, 2024

A new proposal by the US government may change the way green card applicants apply for residency. According to sources, the new rule would require applicants to deposit $100,000 as a guarantee of their intention to stay in the US.

The proposal is part of a broader effort to reform the US immigration system and ensure that only those who are truly committed to living in the US are granted residency.

The new rule would apply to all green card applicants, including those who are already in the US on a visa or those who are applying from abroad.

The proposal is still in its early stages, and it's unclear when or if it will be implemented.
